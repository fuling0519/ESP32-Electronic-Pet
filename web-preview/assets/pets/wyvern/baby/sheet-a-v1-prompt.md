Use the attached purple wyvern as the style and character reference. Draw its BABY version: the same species and the same colorful pixel-art style, outline color, soft upper-left light and palette, but younger and smaller.

Baby design (keep identical in every cell):
- Same round lavender-purple face, same tiny dark bean eyes with one white highlight, same small "w" mouth, pink cheek blush, pink inner ears and cream segmented belly as the reference. Do NOT enlarge the eyes.
- Baby proportions: the head is about 60% of the total height, the body is short and chubby, legs are short stubby feet.
- Horns are only two tiny rounded cream nubs (much shorter than the reference), plus a very small purple forehead bump.
- Wings are tiny, about half the size of the reference wings, attached at the shoulders, cream-peach membranes.
- A short, chubby purple tail with a cream underside, visible beside the right leg.
- Only two arms and two legs; no extra limbs.

Layout: a 4-column x 3-row grid of 12 separate poses on one image. Every cell shows the same baby at the same size, front view, feet on the same baseline near the bottom of the cell, with empty space around it. Nothing overlaps cell borders. No grid lines, no text, no numbers, no ground, no shadows.

Row 1 - idle loop, eyes open, gentle small smile; body and head stay in place, only wings and tail move:
1. Neutral: wings resting at the sides, tail tip down.
2. Wings open slightly outward, tail tip swings a little to the right.
3. Back to neutral like cell 1.
4. Wings fold a little closer to the body, tail tip swings a little to the left.

Row 2 - sad and sick, wings drooping:

6. Same sad pose with eyes closed (worried closed eyes).
7. Sick: tired half-closed eyes, small flat mouth, wings loose and hanging close to the body. Skin stays purple.
8. Same sick pose with eyes fully closed.

Row 3 - happy and eating (no bowl or food drawn):
9. Happy: small closed-mouth smile, cheeks lifted, eyes open, wings slightly spread.
10. Same happy pose with content closed smiling eyes (upside-down U arcs).
11. Eating bite: head tilted down, mouth near chest level as if biting food in front of it.
12. Chewing: head up again, cheeks puffed, eyes open.

Background: one flat solid pure green (#00FF00) everywhere outside the character, no gradient, no texture. Crisp pixel edges.
